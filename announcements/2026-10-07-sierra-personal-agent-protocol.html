🤖 Agentic Commerce · Key story Sierra and Meta introduce Personal Agent Protocol Sierra (Bret Taylor & Clay Bavor) · 6 Oct • An open standard for how personal AI agents sign in to and act with businesses, developed by Sierra and Meta with Genesys, Instinct, Rocket, Shopify, Stripe and Walmart. • Built on OAuth: an agent can browse as a guest, and once the customer signs in they choose read-only or write access. Each company decides whether agents use its website, its APIs (MCP, OpenAPI) or its own company agent. • The v0.1 spec is due later this month. Payments are a future extension, not part of v0.1.
