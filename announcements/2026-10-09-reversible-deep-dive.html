🔄 REVERSIBLE deep dive Agentic commerce · Deep dive What it is: a designer-fashion discovery/price-comparison platform (founded 2022 by Deng Deng, San Jose, no public funding found). Labs is their new B2B arm - an API so agents/apps can plug into their catalog: 700+ retailers, 1m+ products, normalized price/size/shipping/returns data, plus affiliate buy links that earn commission. It's affiliate commerce + data, NOT payment execution. REVERSIBLE Labs How it works for an agent: request access → search natural-language → get normalized products → compare offers by size/price/delivery → hand the shopper a trackable buy link → shopper checks out with the retailer → you earn commission after validation. No card vault, no payment mandate, no autonomous checkout. The honest asterisks: • "Up to 12%" commission is a ceiling, not the standard rate. Actual split/API pricing needs a contract, nothing public. • 700+ retailers couldn't be independently verified, and coverage for a SG shopper will be much smaller. • "Real-time" data actually refreshes at varying intervals, final price confirmed by retailer. • This does NOT invent affiliate APIs - CJ, impact.com, Rakuten all have them. REVERSIBLE's pitch is fashion-specific normalization + ONE affiliate relationship instead of you signing up with every network. CJ developers The Ant lens: the interesting question is who monetizes AI shopping intent BEFORE payment. REVERSIBLE wants agent builders earning affiliate rev on discovery - while Crossmint/Stripe fight over the checkout. Two different layers of the same transaction, both trying to tax it.
