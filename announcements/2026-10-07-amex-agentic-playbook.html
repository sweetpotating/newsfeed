💳 Agentic Commerce · Key story American Express launches Business Playbook for Agentic Commerce American Express press release · 6 Oct • Amex released a free Business Playbook for Agentic Commerce for merchants (getting found by AI, handling AI-driven transactions, trust) and is piloting a Merchant AI Advisory Council. • Its Trendex survey: 83% of US businesses see agentic commerce as an opportunity, but only 41% are experimenting with it and 86% expect to need help from payment providers. • Shoppers are cautious: 52% would let an AI agent buy everyday items, only 15% high-value ones. • It builds on Amex's ACE Developer Kit and Agent Purchase Protection, which covers errors by registered AI agents.
